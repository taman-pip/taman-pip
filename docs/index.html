How to explore

Rotate
Drag with one finger or your mouse.
Zoom
Pinch, scroll, or use the + and − buttons.
Move around
Slide two fingers or choose Pan and drag.
Find a house
Search a username, or tap a name tag on a roof.
Walk around
Choose Walk, then steer with the on-screen stick or W A S D. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
Stall games
Open the Games tab, or visit the Pasar Malam, for four quick games: wrap, flip, toss and gepuk.
Easter eggs
Seven things from the earlier films are hidden here. Look at the sky, take a walk past Pip’s gate, the mansion guardhouse and the bus stop, and try searching for what Pip went shopping for.
Explore as a neighbour
Open a house and choose Explore as. You start at your own gate, the town remembers you, and Share gives you a link to your house.
Visit a place
Select a map pin or a place in the directory.
Keyboard
Focus the map. Arrow keys pan, + and − zoom, and Home returns to the overview.
