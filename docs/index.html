How to explore

Look around
Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom, two fingers (or Pan) to slide.
Find a house
Search a username, or tap a name tag on a roof.
Walk
Tap Walk and steer with the stick or W A S D. Hold Run or Shift to run.
Explore as a neighbour
Open a house and choose Explore as. You start at that gate, and Share gives a link to the house.
Stall games
Four stalls at the Pasar Malam have a game. Open the stall, or walk up to it, to play.
Easter eggs
Eight things from the films are hidden in town. for hints.
Sound
Music starts on your first tap. The speaker button turns it off. The “hus hus hus” is by @thedrizzleshow, used with permission.
